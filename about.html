Our Story

Founded in Gaborone, August 2023

Asset Track, operating under The Telematics Group (Pty) Ltd, was founded in August 2023 with one clear focus: to give fleet and truck owners real visibility over their vehicles and assets, serving the logistics industry across Botswana and the wider region.

We saw operators running blind — unsure where their trucks were, how much fuel they were burning, or how their drivers behaved on the road. We set out to change that.

Over the years since, we've built cutting-edge telematics technology that brings tracking, AI dash cameras, fuel monitoring and cold-chain visibility together into one platform — the kind of tooling that was once reserved for the biggest operators, made accessible to every fleet.
